Supervised regression | Deployed with Streamlit

House Price Prediction

An end-to-end regression pipeline that predicts real estate unit prices from property and location features. A Random Forest Regressor was benchmarked against Ridge Regression and Gradient Boosting after median imputation and StandardScaler scaling. Feature importances show that MRT station distance, house age and geographic coordinates drive price most. The winning pipeline is saved with Joblib and served in a Streamlit app for real-time predictions.

R² = 0.81 on a held-out test set of 83 records
